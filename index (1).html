About JARS

At JARS Hat Manufacturer, we specialize in manufacturing hats and western hats for brands, distributors, wholesalers, and companies looking to develop products according to their own specifications.

Our history has been connected to the hat industry for generations. Over time, our family has evolved from hat trading to manufacturing and later to serving customers in Mexico and the United States, building experience in materials, processes, shapes, finishes, and product development.

Today, from San Francisco del Rincón, Guanajuato, we work with customers who are looking for more than just a supplier. We become involved in the development of each project to understand its target market, product characteristics, available materials, and the details that are part of each brand's identity.
